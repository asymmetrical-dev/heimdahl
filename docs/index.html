A Vision Crisis in Plain Sight

Over 2.2 billion people globally suffer from vision impairment. As we move towards a more digital world—from automotive HUDs to AR glasses—the friction between our eyes and our screens is growing.

Standard displays assume everyone sees the same way. This leads to fatigue, misreads, and safety risks. Heimdahl turns this around by making the technology adapt to the person, not the other way around.
